# VFX asset notices

The files in this directory are embedded into the web player and standalone HTML exports.

- `arcane-parry.png`, `crescent-slash.png`, `electric-impact.png`, `magical-projectile.png`, `radiant-heal.png`, and `rain-field.png` are selected grid sprite sheets from **PVFX Foundry 0.4.0** by nerijs. They are released under CC0 1.0 Universal. The upstream license is preserved as `PVFX-LICENSE.txt`. Source: <https://nerijs.itch.io/pvfx-foundry>
- `rain-overlay.png` is **rain** by gmason, released under CC0. Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/rain-1>
- `blood-splatter-01.png` is **miscBloodDecalParticles - blood_splatter_01.png**, released under CC0. Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/miscblooddecalparticles-bloodsplatter01png>
- `blood-splatter-02.png` is **Blood splat** by TobiasM, released under CC0. Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/blood-splat>
- `bullet-strip.png` is from **Sci-fi Space Simple Bullets**, released under CC0. Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-space-simple-bullets>
- `blood-lens-overlay.png` is **Blood overlay** by 1up Indie, released under CC0. Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/blood-overlay>
- `raindrops-window-cc0.webp` is adapted from **RaindropsOnWindow.jpg** by Raysonho @ Open Grid Scheduler / Grid Engine, released under CC0. The project copy discards the photographed background and keeps the real droplet relief as a transparent light/dark-safe overlay. Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RaindropsOnWindow.jpg>
- `health-splats-cc0.png` is the sparse `Health_Spalts.png` variant from **Health Overlay Images** by GreyFrogGames, released under CC0. Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/health-overlay-images>

The player uses layered cover/crop or edge compositing so overlays remain usable on wide desktop and tall mobile canvases.
